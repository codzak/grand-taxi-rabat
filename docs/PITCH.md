# Pitch notes

Facts checked on 10 Nov 2026. Check dates and amounts again before you quote them.

## The problem in one line

Grand taxis carry a big share of Morocco's daily trips, and almost none of it is online: where stands are, where they go, what it costs, where to get off.

## Numbers

- About 44,650 grand taxis and close to 180,000 taxi drivers in Morocco (Interior Minister, Dec 2025).
- Of Moroccans who use public transport, 47% take grand taxis (L'Économiste / Sunergia survey, June 2025).
- The Interior Ministry is running a study to reform the sector. It lists slow adoption of apps and geolocation among the problems.

## Competitors

| Who | What they do | Our edge |
|---|---|---|
| Blassty (Rabat Région Mobilité, public, Dec 2025) | Official multimodal app: bus, tram, parking, airport taxis, taxi station locations | Grand taxi depth: separate destinations, prices, routes, free drop-off, driver mode. Possible partner |
| Google Maps | General map, a few user-added stands | Data verified on site |
| Uber, Careem, inDrive | Ride-hailing, no legal framework in Morocco | We don't book or price rides |
| Word of mouth | The current source | Same info before leaving home |

What I saw in Blassty as a user: destinations crammed into station names, duplicates, no search by destination, overlapping pins, no routes. We never copy its data.

6. UM6P StartGate Rabat: eligibility not found, check their site.

## What to bring

- The PDF (`cahier-des-charges-grand-taxi-rabat.pdf`).
- The mockup link (share it first, it is private by default).
- Real data: verified stands, at least one recorded route.
- A pilot story: a few drivers who tried driver mode.

## Business model: ideas to test, no numbers yet

- Data partnership or licence with RRM and the communes.
- Anonymous demand data per line, useful for the taxi reform.
- Discreet local ads near stands, no tracking.
- Phase 2: a small fee on in-app card payments.
